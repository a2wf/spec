What this validator does

  • Structural validation against the A2WF v1.1 JSON Schema.
  • Semantic checks beyond the schema: Section 7.7 oversight directives, level conformance, module-claim consistency, rate-limit references, profile-claim correctness.
  • Forbidden-pattern detection: numeric conformance scores, legal-compliance phrasing.
  • SHOULD recommendations reported as WARN.
  • v1.0 documents accepted with a deprecation warning per spec Section 12.

What this validator does NOT do

  • It does not certify legal compliance with GDPR, the EU AI Act, or any regulation.
  • It does not verify that the document accurately reflects the operator's actual site policy.
  • It does not check audit-trail integrity, factual data-handling claims, or jurisdiction correctness.
  • It does not upload or store the file. The validation runs entirely in your browser.
